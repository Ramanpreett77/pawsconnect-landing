Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

How do you verify pet sitters? ▼

Every sitter undergoes a comprehensive 5-step verification: (1) Government ID verification, (2) Criminal background check, (3) Reference verification, (4) In-person or video interview, (5) Home safety assessment. Only 15% of applicants are accepted to ensure the highest quality care for your pet.

What if my pet has special needs or medication? ▼

Many of our sitters specialize in pets with special needs, medications, or behavioral challenges. You can filter search results to find sitters with specific expertise, and all care instructions are clearly communicated before booking. During the free meet and greet, you can demonstrate medication routines and discuss specific requirements.

Is there really insurance coverage? ▼

Yes! Every booking through PawsConnect is covered by premium pet care insurance up to $25,000. This covers veterinary care costs in case of illness or injury while under a sitter's care. Traditional kennels often have limited or no insurance coverage.

What if I need to cancel or change my booking? ▼

We offer flexible cancellation policies. Cancel up to 7 days before for a full refund, or 2-7 days before for a 50% refund. For last-minute emergencies, contact our 24/7 support team who can help you reschedule or find alternative care options.

How much does it cost? ▼

Prices vary by service and location, but typically range from $25-50 per day for pet sitting/boarding, $15-30 per walk for dog walking, and $30-45 per day for house sitting. This is often 40-60% less expensive than traditional kennels, with much more personalized care.

Can I meet the sitter before booking? ▼

Absolutely! We encourage and offer FREE meet and greets. This allows you to meet the sitter, see their home, introduce your pet, ask questions, and ensure it's the perfect match. There's no obligation to book, and you can meet with multiple sitters if you'd like.
